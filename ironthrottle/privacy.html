Privacy Policy - Iron Throttle

Iron Throttle: Bike Racing (Android, com.ironthrottle.racing)
Effective date: 16 November 2026

Your game data stays on your device

Iron Throttle saves your career progress, coins, bikes, upgrades, missions, settings and the birth year you enter on the first screen only on your device. There is no account or sign-up. The developer does not run servers for the game and does not collect, receive or sell any personal data. We never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Age screen

On first launch the game asks for your birth year. It is used only on your device to choose safe settings for your age group, and it is never sent anywhere.

Ads

The current version of the game shows no ads from any ad network. The roadside billboards in the game are built-in artwork and send no data.

A future version may offer optional reward videos from Google AdMob (watched only if you choose to, after a race, never while racing). If that happens, this policy will be updated before the release, and:

  • players under 13 (or who have not entered their age) will get no ad videos and no tracking;
  • players under 16 will only get non-personalised ads;
  • all ads will be limited to content rated for general audiences;
  • in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message will ask for your choice first.

When ads are shown, AdMob may collect your device advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data. See how Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

The game has no in-app purchases.

Children

Iron Throttle is a motorbike combat racing game made for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Either one deletes all game data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
