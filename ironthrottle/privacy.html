Privacy Policy - Iron Throttle

Iron Throttle: Bike Racing (Android, com.ironthrottle.racing)
Effective date: 16 November 2026

Your game data stays on your device

Iron Throttle saves your career progress, coins, bikes, upgrades, missions, settings and the birth year you enter on the first screen only on your device. There is no account or sign-up. The developer does not run servers for the game and does not collect, receive or sell any personal data. The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob collects when ads are shown (see Ads). We never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Age screen

On first launch the game asks for your birth year. It is used only on your device to choose safe settings for your age group, and it is never sent anywhere.

Ads

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob, only outside of races:

  • Reward videos are optional: you choose to watch one after a race (double coins) or after a crash (one continue).
  • Full-screen ads may appear between races, at most once every 3 races, never during a race.
  • Players under 13 (or who have not entered their age) get no ads from any ad network and no tracking.
  • Players under 16 only get non-personalised ads. All ads are limited to content rated for general audiences (G).
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice before personalised ads. You can change it anytime in Settings → Privacy & Ads → Privacy choices.

When ads are shown, AdMob may collect your device advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information and ad interaction data, to show, personalise (16+ with consent only), measure ads and prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

The roadside billboards in the game are built-in artwork and send no data.

In-app purchases

The game has no in-app purchases.

Children

Iron Throttle is a motorbike combat racing game made for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13. If a player enters an age under 13, the game requests no ads from any ad network and collects no personal information.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Either one deletes all game data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
