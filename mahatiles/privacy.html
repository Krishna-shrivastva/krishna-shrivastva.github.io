Privacy Policy - MahaTiles

MahaTiles: Mahjong Tile Match (Android, com.krishna.mahatiles)
Effective date: 14 October 2026

Your game data stays on your phone

MahaTiles saves your level progress, stars, coins, collected tile sets, settings and play statistics only on your phone. There is no account or sign-up. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any personal data.

Ads

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data to show, personalise and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice anytime in Settings → Ad privacy choices. See how Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Watching a rewarded ad is always optional.

In-app purchases

If you buy "Remove Ads", the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details.

Game updates

The app may check for and download small game updates from the developer's update server. Like any web request, this shares your IP address with the hosting provider. No personal data is sent.

Children

MahaTiles is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset progress in the app, or uninstall the app. Either one deletes all game data from your phone. For data collected by Google AdMob, you can reset your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
