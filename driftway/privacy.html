Privacy Policy - Driftway

Driftway: Car Parking Puzzle (Android, com.krishna.driftway)
Effective date: 16 November 2026

Your game data stays on your phone

Driftway saves your levels cleared, stars, hints, lives, daily missions, race stats and settings only on your phone. There is no account or sign-up, and the game never asks for your name, email or a password. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any personal data.

Race mode in this version is against practice bots. It runs fully offline and sends nothing.

Ads

Driftway is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC):

  • Rewarded videos you choose to watch, for an extra hint, heart, life or undos (at most 8 a day). Watching is always optional.
  • A small banner on the Home screen and the level list. It never shows while you play.
  • An occasional full-screen ad when you tap Next after a level (at most one every 3 levels) or Try again after losing one: never before level 15, never in races, and never more often than every 2½ minutes.

The game asks AdMob for family-friendly ads only (content rating G) and marks its ad requests for users under the age of consent, so AdMob shows non-personalised ads and limits how it uses data. When an ad is requested, AdMob may still collect your device advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Data is encrypted in transit. See how Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before ads load, and you can change your choice anytime in Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

Driftway is a general-audience puzzle game for players aged 13 and over. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all game data from your phone. The developer holds no copy. For data collected by Google AdMob, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Changes

If Driftway adds online features (such as racing other players or cloud saves), or if this policy changes for any other reason, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date before the change ships.
